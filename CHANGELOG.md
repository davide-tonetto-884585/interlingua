## 0.1.0

First release.

- A translation above each line of the book, starting where each sentence
  begins, and a translated paragraph after each paragraph as an option.
- Translate a book chapter by chapter ("Translate current chapter"), or a
  few pages from the page shown (5, 10 or 20); a page that is not
  translated asks what to translate, and a "No" keeps it quiet until you
  translate from the menu.
- Google or DeepL, chosen in Reading settings, Interlingua, each with your
  own key kept under "Passwords and keys". The Google service is Chrome's
  unofficial endpoint; DeepL is the official API.
- Translations are kept on the card, a chapter at a time.
- A tap on the icon after a translated sentence opens it whole.
- Settings for the translation's size, room, font, tone and colour, the
  style of the translated paragraph, the closed line and the language.
- Interface in English, Italian and German.
